01 / THE HONEST MATH

Bare skin ages fastest. The drive is longest.

Those two facts pull against each other. Stubble over bare scalp shows within days, and the lowest part of the blend softens first, so a skin fade is the cut that most wants a quick touch-up. From Roswell a quick touch-up is 92 minutes each way.

There are three ways to handle that, and I'll help you pick: come down often and keep it razor sharp; accept the grown-in look for part of each cycle; or let me start the fade a little higher and blend it longer, so it looks intentional as it grows.

02 / HEAT AT BOTH ENDS

You're driving into warmer air.

Roswell averages about 33 days a year at 100°F or more; Carlsbad, where you'll be sitting in my chair, averages 48 (Open-Meteo, 2023–25). Bare sides are the cut people reach for when the heat is like that, because there's nothing on the sides to hold sweat.

04 / QUESTIONS

Skin fade questions from the north end of 285.

Low, mid or high if I can only come down now and then?+

Low or mid. Less exposed scalp means the regrowth has somewhere to hide. High is the boldest on the day and the first to look due.

Can I book my next skin fade before I drive home?+

Yes, and I'd suggest it. Open Booksy while you're still in Carlsbad and grab the date that matches how sharp you like to keep it.

What should I show you if I want the one with the hard part and beard?+

Point at it on the skin fade page; that cut is from my chair. Then tell me how tall you want the fade.
