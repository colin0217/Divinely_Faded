ROSWELL / ONE ROAD SOUTH

Coming from Roswell.

Divinely Faded is in Carlsbad, 76 miles south of Roswell. It's US 285 the whole way, through Artesia at about the halfway mark, and it takes around 92 minutes.

Here's what to sort out before you head south: what to book, when I'm open and exactly where the shop is.

GET DIRECTIONS
01 / THE ROUTE

Straight down 285.

Take US 285 south out of Roswell and stay on it. Artesia comes up about halfway; keep going and you're in Carlsbad.

The shop is at 303 S Halagueno St, Carlsbad, NM 88220. Tap Get Directions above and Google Maps handles the last few turns.

Leave a little slack for town traffic at the Carlsbad end. If you're going to be late, text me at (575) 706-3095.

Heading down from Roswell.

How far is Divinely Faded from Roswell?+

About 76 miles, or around 92 minutes south on US 285.

Do I drive through Artesia?+

Yes. US 285 runs straight through Artesia, roughly halfway between Roswell and Carlsbad.

Can I book the same day?+

Only if Booksy shows an open time that day. Check before you leave Roswell, since I work by appointment only.

What do I pay with?+

Cash, card, Zelle or Cash App, after the service.
