ROSWELL / LOW TAPER FADE

Low taper fade, from Roswell.

If I had to recommend one cut to someone who lives 76 miles from my chair, it would be this one. A low taper trims only the sideburns, the hair around the ear and the neckline, and leaves the rest alone, so there's very little to grow back wrong. I cut it in Carlsbad at 303 S Halagueno St, Carlsbad, NM 88220; from Roswell that's US 285 south through Artesia, about 92 minutes (OpenStreetMap routing).

Here's the case for the taper on a long road, the one place it loses to a skin fade, and what to book.

01 / WHY IT TRAVELS WELL

The fewest trips for the cleanest edges.

Every other fade on this site goes somewhere near skin on the whole side of the head, and skin is where regrowth shows first. A low taper exposes almost none. The sideburn and the nape blur a little as the weeks pass, but from across a room the cut still looks like the cut. For someone facing a 92-minute drive each way, that's the difference between coming down when you want to and coming down because you have to.

02 / THE SUMMER TRADE-OFF

Cover on the sides, heat on the sides.

Roswell sees about 33 days a year at 100°F or more, and a July high around 97°F (Open-Meteo, 2023–25). A taper keeps hair over most of the side of the head, so it holds a little more warmth than a skin fade does. If you work outside through that stretch and want bare sides for the summer, book a skin fade and switch back to the taper in the fall, when the slower grow-out matters more than the heat.

04 / QUESTIONS

Taper questions from Roswell.

Is a low taper a good first cut to try with a barber this far away?+

Yes. It changes the least, so if we're still learning what you like, nothing about it is hard to live with on the drive home. The low taper fade page shows the side and back views.

Low taper or mid taper for someone who visits rarely?+

Low. It shows regrowth later. Mid looks sharper on the day and asks for a return a little sooner.

Can I keep my hair long on top with a taper?+

That's the point of it. The taper tidies the edges and leaves the top to you.
