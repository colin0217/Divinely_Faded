ROSWELL / HAIR DESIGNS

Hair designs, from Roswell.

A design is a line or curve cut into the shortest part of a fade, and I cut them at my shop in Carlsbad, 76 miles south of Roswell on US 285. On Booksy it's a $10 add-on to whatever haircut you book.

The catch with a design is that it lives in the hair that grows back first, so from 92 minutes away it pays to think about timing before you think about shape.

01 / TIMING

Cut it for the date, not for the month.

Because the lines sit where the fade is shortest, they blur as that stubble fills in. If you're driving 92 minutes down US 285 (OpenStreetMap routing), book the design close to whatever you want it sharp for: a game, a party, photos. Leave it longer and the design is the first thing to go, which is fine if you only wanted it once.

Bring a picture, or tell me where you want it and how bold, and we'll settle the shape in the chair.

04 / QUESTIONS

Designs from Roswell, answered.

How much is a hair design?+

$10 on top of the haircut you book. The Luxury Grooming Experience includes a basic one.

How long does a design last?+

It softens as the short hair around it grows back. How long that takes depends on your hair, which is why I'd time it to an event rather than count on weeks.

Can I book the design without a haircut?+

It's an add-on, so it goes with a cut. Text me at (575) 706-3095 if you're unsure how to pair it.
