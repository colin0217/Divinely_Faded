01 / SIBLINGS

One in four, so bring them all.

A quarter of Roswell is under 18, 25% by the Census (ACS 2023), so many homes have more than one head to cut. Booksy has no separate kids' service, which means each child books a regular haircut in their own slot. Pick slots that touch each other, put yours on the end, and the whole family is done in one visit instead of two drives.

I work alone, so the kid who was nervous last time sees the same face this time.

02 / WHAT TO BOOK

The Essential, with a picture.

For most kids the Essential Haircut is the booking: $50, with 40 min held on Booksy. A low fade with a line-up, a taper under a longer top, or curls over a skin fade all fit inside that time. Lines cut in are a $10 add-on.

Save a photo on your phone before you leave Roswell. Showing me the cut beats describing it, especially with a kid who isn't sure what to call it.

04 / PARENTS ASK

From Roswell, with kids.

Can two kids share one appointment?+

Each child needs their own slot so I can give both the full time. Book them back to back.

What's the drive like with kids from Roswell?+

About 92 minutes each way on US 285, with Artesia roughly halfway if anyone needs a stop. The shop is at 303 S Halagueno St, Carlsbad, NM 88220.

When are you open on weekends?+

Saturdays, 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. With about 8,538 kids in K–12 school in Roswell (Census ACS 2023), that Saturday window is how families avoid missing class for a 92-minute drive. Closed Wednesdays and Sundays.
