01 / CURLS IN DRY AIR

Less frizz, more thirst.

Roswell's air averages around 39% humidity (Open-Meteo, 2023–25). For curly hair that cuts both ways: damp-climate frizz mostly isn't your problem, but dry curls lose their spring and look dull faster. When I shape the top I cut for how your curl behaves dry, not wet, and I'll tell you how much length your pattern needs to keep its bounce.

02 / A FADE THAT WAITS FOR YOU

Curls forgive. Skin doesn't.

Growth on top just reads as more curl. Growth on faded sides is what gives the cut away, and from Roswell every touch-up is another 92 minutes behind the wheel. If you can't come back often, say so: a low or mid fade keeps more hair on the sides and softens as it grows, where a skin-tight version looks due much sooner.

04 / QUESTIONS

Curly questions from up the road.

Should I wash my curls before the drive down from Roswell?+

Come with your hair the way you normally wear it, dry. I want to see the real curl before I cut, and 92 minutes in the car is enough for it to settle.

Can a kid from Roswell get a curly top fade?+

Yes. Kids are welcome, and parents choose the Essential Haircut for them on Booksy. If a child is nervous about clippers, a low fade is a gentle place to start.

Which fade height holds up longest between visits?+

Low, then mid, then skin. The less bare scalp I expose, the slower the grow-out shows. The curly top fade page shows the skin version so you can compare.
