01 / SCHOOL AGES AND UP

A cut that works at fifteen and at forty.

Roswell is 25% under 18 (Census ACS 2023), so a lot of households reading this have a teenager who wants the front forward and the sides clean. The textured fringe is a good fit for that age because it needs no comb and no part, and it looks deliberate even after a day at school. A parent can get the same cut with a lower, heavier fringe and it reads completely grown up.

Each person books their own appointment on Booksy. Put a teen's slot right after yours so one 92-minute drive takes care of both.

02 / HOW HEAVY IN FRONT

Lighter for summer.

Hair on the forehead is hair on the forehead in a 97°F July, and Roswell runs about 33 days a year at 100°F or hotter (Open-Meteo, 2023–25). When it's that hot, I'd leave the fringe lighter and shorter, like the first photo on my styles page, so it sits off the skin. Come fall, the longer, heavier sweep is comfortable again.

04 / QUESTIONS

Fringe questions from Roswell.

When should I book the next one from this far away?+

When the front starts touching your eyes, the shape is finished; the taper on the sides usually goes a little before that. Book on Booksy as soon as the sides look soft, so the trip lands before the fringe does.

Is this the same as an edgar?+

Cousins. An edgar has a blunt, straight line across the forehead and a sharper fade; a textured fringe is softer and broken up at the ends. Bring a photo of whichever you mean, or point at the textured fringe page.

My kid has a cowlick at the front. Will a fringe work?+

Sometimes not; a strong front cowlick pushes hair up instead of forward. I'll check it in the chair and suggest a crop or a taper if the fringe would fight it.
