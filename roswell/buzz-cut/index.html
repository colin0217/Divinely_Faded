ROSWELL / BUZZ CUT

Buzz cut, from Roswell.

Let me be straight with you: nobody should drive 76 miles for a guard-length buzz and nothing else. I'm in Carlsbad, at 303 S Halagueno St, Carlsbad, NM 88220, which from Roswell is US 285 through Artesia and about 92 minutes each way (OpenStreetMap routing).

What makes the trip reasonable is what I do around the buzz: a squared line-up, a fade up the sides so it never looks like a home job, and a razor at the neck. And it pays off more if you stack the visit.

01 / MAKE THE DRIVE COUNT

Stack it.

Roswell is a city of 47,823 people (Census ACS 2023) with its own barbers, so if you're coming to me for a short cut, give the day a second reason. Book the Signature so the beard gets done under the same hot towel, or put a facial or a straight razor shave right after the cut.

Kids with buzz cuts fit the same plan. Each child gets their own appointment on Booksy, booked back to back with yours, and everyone rides home finished.

02 / THE SUMMER CASE

Short hair in a long hot stretch.

A July afternoon in Roswell typically tops out at 97°F, and the city sees roughly 33 days a year at 100°F or more (Open-Meteo, 2023–25). A buzz is the cut that asks nothing of you on those days: no product melting, no shape to fix, a wet head dries in minutes.

The grow-out is kind too: only the fade and the line-up blur, so the gap between trips south can stretch.

04 / QUESTIONS

Short cuts, long drive.

Which guard should I ask for on a buzz cut?+

Tell me how much scalp you're happy to show and whether you want it the same all over or faded on the sides. I'll pick the number from there. The buzz cut page explains the buzz, crew and high and tight family.

Can two of us from Roswell book back to back?+

Yes, as long as Booksy shows two open times in a row. Book them both before you leave; I don't hold unbooked gaps.

How long will a buzz with a fade look sharp?+

The edges decide that, not the top. Ask me in the chair and I'll give you my honest guess for your hair and the height of your fade.
