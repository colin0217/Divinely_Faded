HOBBS / COLOR & PERM

Color and perms, from Hobbs.

A Color Transformation is the longest booking on my menu, and a Perm isn't far behind. Both happen in my chair in Carlsbad, 70 miles from Hobbs along US 62/180, about 80 minutes by car (OpenStreetMap routing).

Of everything I do, these two are the appointments that give a long drive a reason: one trip, one big change.

02 / PLAN THE DAY

Give it the day.

Add the drive both ways to 4 hr in the chair and the day is spoken for. Book a morning slot, eat before you leave Hobbs, and don't put anything behind it.

A Perm is shorter, but still long enough that I'd give that day nothing else except the trip home.

03 / THE SUMMER

Lighter hair, milder heat.

Hobbs runs a touch cooler than Carlsbad: an average July high of 94°F and about 23 days a year at 100°F or more, against 98°F and 48 here (Open-Meteo, 2023–25). Bleached hair and a fresh perm both ask for some care in the sun and heat, so if you're planning the change for summer, that's one small thing in your favor.

Send me a photo of the shade or the wave you want by text before you book, so we agree on where it lands before you drive. Color stays on the head; I don't dye beards.
