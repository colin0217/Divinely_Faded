HOBBS / BRAIDS

Braids, worth the drive from Hobbs.

Braids are the one appointment where the trip from Hobbs feels short. Booksy holds 2 hr for a set, and the drive to my chair in Carlsbad is about 80 minutes, 70 miles west on US 62/180 (OpenStreetMap routing).

Every set is built for the person in the chair, over a fade if you want one, so the planning starts before you leave Lea County.

01 / BEFORE THE DRIVE

Text the photo first.

Booksy lists braids with a price that varies by style. Send me a picture of the set you're after at (575) 706-3095 before booking, so you know the price and I know what rows to plan. Doing that from Hobbs saves a drive that ends in a surprise.

Then pick a slot with nothing after it. The 2 hr Booksy blocks plus the road back is most of a day.

03 / THE ROAD

Make the hours count.

The average commute in Hobbs is 24.9 minutes (Census ACS 2023). The drive here is more than three of those, one way. That's why it's worth asking, when you text, about a fade on the sides in the same visit: clean skin makes the rows stand out, and you're not back on 62/180 for a separate cut.

Hobbs gets about 23 days a year at 100°F or more (Open-Meteo, 2023–25), and some people like faded sides under the rows for that reason alone: less hair on the neck through the hot months.

Payment is after the braids are finished: cash, card, Zelle or Cash App. Cancel early on Booksy if plans change; a late cancellation on a booking this long may carry a cancellation fee.
