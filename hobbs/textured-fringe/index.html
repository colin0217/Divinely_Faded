HOBBS / TEXTURED FRINGE

Textured fringe, from Hobbs.

Hair worn forward onto the forehead, the ends broken up so they fall in pieces, the sides tapered. I cut it in Carlsbad; anyone starting in Hobbs has 70 miles of US 62/180 ahead of them, around 80 minutes.

It's a cut people ask for by name, edgar included, and the make-up of the city has something to do with that.

01 / THE AGE

A twenties cut in a twenties town.

Roughly 23.4% of Hobbs is between 20 and 34 (Census ACS 2023), a slightly bigger slice than Carlsbad's 22%. Fringe cuts, from the soft textured version to the sharper edgar with its blunt line, skew young, so that's a city where I'd expect the question to come up.

Bring the photo. A textured fringe and an edgar are close enough cousins that the picture settles what words can't.

02 / THE CLOCK

When it hits your eyes, you're due.

Once the fringe is in your eyes, the shape is gone. Since the next cut is 70 miles away, I can set it a little higher on the forehead than you'd wear it day to day; it grows into your preferred length over the following weeks and buys time before the next drive.

04 / QUESTIONS

Fringe questions from Hobbs.

Textured fringe or edgar: what do I tell you when I sit down?+

Show me the photo. An edgar has a blunt, straight line across the forehead and a sharp fade; a textured fringe is softer and choppier at the ends. Either is fine; I just want us picturing the same cut.

My son wants the edgar. What do I book from Hobbs?+

An Essential Haircut in his name on Booksy. Kids are welcome, and if you want a cut too, take the slot next to his.

Can I see the three versions first?+

The textured fringe page has a light, a heavy and a back view, all from my chair.
