HOBBS / BUZZ CUT

Buzz cut, from Hobbs.

One clipper length over the whole head, a fade climbing the sides and a squared-off line-up at the front. I cut it in Carlsbad, roughly 80 minutes (OpenStreetMap routing) from a driveway in Hobbs by way of US 62/180.

If your week belongs to a rig, a plant or a field, this is the cut that needs the least from you between visits.

01 / THE DRIVE

Even growth means a longer gap.

When every haircut costs you 80 minutes each direction, the cut that grows out evenly wins. On a buzz the top barely changes as it lengthens; what blurs is the fade and the squared front, and I'll tell you in the chair roughly when they'll start to go.

About 17.8% of employed Hobbs residents work in mining, oil and gas or agriculture (Census ACS 2023). On a rotation, book the trip for a day off rather than after a shift; Saturday runs 8 AM to 2 PM.

02 / UNDER A HARD HAT

Nothing to flatten, nothing to fix.

A buzz comes out from under a hard hat the way it went in: no part to re-set, no top to lift. All that matters after a shift is whether the outline is still clean.

04 / QUESTIONS

Buzz cut questions from Lea County.

How often will I be making the drive from Hobbs for a buzz cut?+

Less often than for most cuts, since the length grows evenly; the fade and the line-up set the pace. Ask me in the chair.

Buzz, crew or high and tight for shift work?+

Any of the three. A crew cut keeps a bit more on top if you're unsure about your head shape; a high and tight shows the outline most.
