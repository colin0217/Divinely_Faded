02 / BEFORE YOU LEAVE LEA COUNTY

A short checklist.

Book first. I only work by appointment, and Booksy is where every open time shows up.

Hobbs and Carlsbad are both on Mountain Time, so the time on your booking is the time on your phone.

Saturday is 8 AM to 2 PM. On weekdays I take appointments Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday.

If the drive runs long, text me at (575) 706-3095 before your appointment time, not after.
