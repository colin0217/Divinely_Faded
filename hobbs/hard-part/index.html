HOBBS / HARD PART

Hard part, from Hobbs.

A hard part is a part line shaved into the hair so it never needs combing in. I cut it in Carlsbad, at 303 S Halagueno St, Carlsbad, NM 88220; for anyone leaving Hobbs that means US 62/180 heading west for close to 80 minutes.

The line fills in faster than anything else on the head, so this is a cut you time rather than keep on a routine.

01 / TIMING

Cut it as close to the date as you can.

A shaved line has no hair to hide regrowth, so it blurs before the fade around it. If you're covering 70 miles for a wedding, a graduation or photos, book it in the last day or two before, not the week before. At about 80 minutes each way (OpenStreetMap routing), a Saturday morning slot has you back in Hobbs by early afternoon with the line at its sharpest.

No event? Let the line soften into the cut and decide next visit whether to shave it again.

02 / PLACEMENT

Send me the side before you leave.

The line should follow the side your hair already falls toward, or you'll fight it every morning. Text a photo of your hair after a shower to (575) 706-3095 before you set out and I'll plan the placement before you arrive.

As late as you can manage. A day or two before is ideal, since the shaved line is the first thing to grow in.
