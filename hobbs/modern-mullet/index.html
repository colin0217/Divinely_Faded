HOBBS / MODERN MULLET

Modern mullet, from Hobbs.

Tight faded sides, a textured top and honest length in back. I cut modern mullets in Carlsbad; from Hobbs it's US 62/180 the whole way, 70 miles and about 80 minutes by OpenStreetMap routing.

A mullet is grown as much as it's cut, so from Lea County it's a project spread over several trips. This page is about spacing them.

01 / THE GROW

Several trips, one tail.

Starting from short hair, the back needs months to clear the collar while the sides stay tight. Each shaping visit is another 80 minutes each way, so I'd rather space them out than see you every time the fade softens: let the sides go a little looser and come in when the back needs its shape corrected.

Once the tail is where you want it, the sides set the pace again.

02 / COLOR DAY

Platinum turns the drive into a day.

If you want the platinum version on the main mullet page, the Color Transformation runs 4 hr. Add the cut and you've filled most of an afternoon, the kind of appointment that justifies a 70-mile trip. Text (575) 706-3095 before booking cut and color together so I can block the time in one piece.

04 / QUESTIONS

Mullet questions from Hobbs.

How many trips from Hobbs does growing a mullet take?+

Depends on your starting length. From a short cut, expect a few shaping visits while the back catches up; we'll map them out at the first one.

Can I get the cut and the platinum color on one trip?+

Yes, if you text me first so I can book the two back to back. Plan on the better part of an afternoon.

Where can I see both versions?+

The modern mullet page has the platinum one and the dark, curly burst fade mullet.
