What Is a Hot Towel Shave? What Happens in the Chair

A hot towel shave is a traditional wet shave. Hot towels go on first to soften the beard and warm the skin, then shaving cream and a straight razor take the hair off, and an aftershave finishes it. At my shop that's the Straight Razor Beard Shave: $40 for 40 min on Booksy.

Here's each step in order, what the heat actually does for the shave, and which other services bring a hot towel into the visit.

By Tim Murillo

01 / STEP BY STEP

In order.

Towel, lather, razor, finish.

Settle back

The chair reclines and your head rests back, like the client in the photo at the top. Open the collar or tuck it down so the towel can wrap the jaw and throat.

The towels

Hot towels wrap the lower half of the face and stay there for a while. Warmth and moisture work into the whiskers, and that is what makes them easier to cut.

The lather

Shaving cream goes on over the damp beard. It keeps the skin slick, so the blade glides across it instead of catching.

The razor

Then the straight razor, with the skin held smooth under it. This is the part to stay still for, and it is the reason a proper shave is never hurried.

The finish

A soothing aftershave goes on last, over skin that has just been shaved clean.

Run a hand along your jaw before you sit up. That smooth feel is what the towels, the lather and the slow razor work were for.

02 / WHY HEAT

What the towel does.

Softer whiskers, an easier pass.

Dry whiskers are stiff. Warm water softens them, so the razor slices through instead of tugging. That is the real benefit people mean by a hot towel shave: less pulling, less scraping and a closer result on the first pass.

It's also the quietest part of any visit. Eyes closed, face warm, and nobody needs anything from you for a minute or two.

A hot towel is not a medical treatment. If your skin is irritated, broken out or sore, tell me before we start, and take anything that won't settle to a doctor.
