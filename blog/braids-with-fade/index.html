Braids with a Fade: How to Pair Them

Braids with a fade means braiding the hair on top and fading the back and sides short, so every row sits against a clean edge. The two have to be planned together, because the fade decides where the braids can go. At my shop, braids are a 2 hr booking with a price that varies by style, so text me a photo first and ask how to fit the fade into the same visit.

Here's how fade height changes the braided area, which patterns suit which fade, and how to plan the appointment.

By Tim Murillo

01 / HOW THEY FIT

The fade sets the frame.

Rows on top, short on the sides.

Braids need hair to grip. Wherever the fade takes the hair down short, there's nothing left to braid, so the fade line becomes the border of the braided area.

That's why the pairing works so well. The fade gives every row a clean edge, and the contrast between tight braids and bare skin is what makes the set stand out from across a room.

In the photo at the top, two cornrows run back along the side and finish in a gold cuff, with the hair under them faded all the way to skin.

02 / FADE HEIGHT

Low, mid or high.

Each one moves the edge.

High fade

The braids stay on the top of the head. Most of the side is skin, so the rows read bold and narrow, like a strip running front to back.

Mid fade

The braided area reaches a little further down the sides. It sits between the two: some contrast, some coverage.

Low fade or taper

Most of the side keeps its hair, so the rows can wrap further down toward the ear, and the fade turns into a thin, tidy edge at the very bottom.

SKIN FADE STYLE
03 / PATTERNS

Which braids, which fade.

Match the pattern to the edge.

Rows running straight back are the easiest match for any fade height. They line up with the fade and finish neatly at the back of the head.

Curved rows, or rows that come together at the crown, need more hair on the sides to turn in. A lower fade gives them that room; a high fade can cut a curve off before it finishes.

Thick rows or thin ones? Fewer, thicker braids stay readable on a narrow strip above a high fade. More, thinner rows look their sharpest across a wider area, which a lower fade leaves you.

Both done in one trip.

05 / AS IT GROWS

They age differently.

The fade softens first.

Short hair shows regrowth quickly, so the fade is the first part of the look to blur. The braids hold their shape longer, which means the edge under them goes soft while the rows still look neat.

Braiding for an event, a game or photos? Book close to the date, so the fade and the rows are both at their sharpest on the day that counts.
