Essential, Signature or Luxury: Which Service Should You Book?

Book the Essential Haircut ($50, 40 min) when all you need is the cut. Book the Signature Cut ($65, 45 min) when your beard or line-up needs work too. Book the Luxury Grooming Experience ($80, 50 min) when you want the whole treatment in one sitting.

All three start with the same precision haircut. What changes is everything I do around it, and this guide lays that out piece by piece.

By Tim Murillo

01 / THE THREE

Side by side.

Same cut underneath. More layered on top.

Essential Haircut$50 · 40 min+

The haircut itself, shaped around your style and your head, then a straight razor along the neck and a finished style. Nothing else gets added unless you add it at booking.

Book this service
Signature Cut$65 · 45 min+

That same cut, plus your beard trimmed or lined up, a hot towel to finish and straight razor detailing around the edges. Booksy lists it as my most popular service.

Book this service
Luxury Grooming Experience$80 · 50 min+

It opens with a consultation. After the cut come a beard sculpt, a wash with scalp massage, facial steam, two hot towels, razor detailing, a basic hair design, ear and nose waxing, styling and a fragrance to close.

The add-ons are priced on their own: a hair design is $10, a wash and scalp massage $10, nose and ear waxing $10, eyebrows $5 and a fragrance finish $5. If you catch yourself stacking three or four extras onto a Signature Cut, compare that total with the Luxury on Booksy before you pick.

03 / HOW TO DECIDE

Three questions.

Answer them in order.

Does your beard need me?

If not, the Essential covers you. If you want it trimmed, lined up or brought back in line with a fresh fade, that is the Signature. The Essential has no beard work in it, so book up when the beard is part of the plan.

How much time do you have?

Booksy blocks 40 min, 45 min or 50 min. On a lunch break, the first two fit easier. The Luxury is the one to choose on a day you aren't watching the clock.

Regular cut or an occasion?

For your regular cut, the Essential or Signature does the job. Before a wedding, an interview or a photo you will keep for years, the Luxury gives you the full reset: beard, skin, scent and a simple design if you want one.

COMPARE THE HAIRCUTS
04 / BEARD ONLY

No haircut needed?

Two shaves stand on their own.

Want the beard shaved off instead of trimmed? The Straight Razor Beard Shave ($40, 40 min) is booked by itself, with no haircut attached.

Taking the head and the beard down smooth together is the Head & Beard Shave, $80 for 1 hr. Both lean on hot towels and finish with an aftershave.

05 / STILL TORN

Can't decide?

Start in the middle.

If you can't choose, the Signature Cut sits in the middle. It covers the cut and the beard, adds a hot towel, and Booksy lists it as my most popular service. You can always book up or down next time once you know what you like.

On Booksy, choose the service first and the time second. The prices and times in this post are the ones Booksy shows. If plans change after you book, cancel early, because a no-show or late cancellation may carry a fee.

06 / QUESTIONS

Quick answers.

What is a signature haircut?+

At my shop it is the Signature Cut: a precision haircut with a beard trim or line-up, a hot towel finish and straight razor detailing, for $65. Other shops use the name differently, so check what is included wherever you book.

What makes a haircut a luxury haircut?+

Time and extras. My Luxury Grooming Experience runs 50 min and wraps the cut in a consultation, a beard sculpt, a wash, steam, hot towels, waxing, a basic design and a fragrance finish.

Which one should I book for my kid?+

Kids book from the same menu. The Essential Haircut covers a kid's cut, and lines or a curve can be added for $10.

Do I pay before or after?+

After. Payment is due in full once the service is done, by cash, card, Zelle or Cash App.

How long is the Luxury Grooming Experience?+

Booksy holds 50 min for it, against 45 min for the Signature and 40 min for the Essential.
