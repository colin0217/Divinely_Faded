What Is a Drop Fade? And How It Differs from a Burst

A drop fade is a fade whose line dips as it travels around the head. It sits at one height over the sides, then curves down behind the ear and finishes lower at the nape, so from the side the fade line looks like an arc dropping toward the neck.

That curve is the whole difference. Here's how it compares with a regular straight-line fade and with a burst fade, and how to book one with me.

By Tim Murillo

01 / THE SHAPE

Follow the line.

Level at the sides, lower in back.

Trace where the short hair meets the longer hair on a regular fade and the line travels around the head at one height, like a band. On a drop fade that same line starts at the temple, holds level over the ear, then bends down behind it and keeps descending toward the nape.

The bend follows the natural curve of the skull behind the ear. Because of it, the fade frames the ear instead of slicing straight across behind it, and the shape reads rounder from the side.

03 / DROP VS BURST

Drop or burst.

Both curve. Differently.

A burst fade curves around the ear in a semicircle and then fades back out, so the hair behind it stays long. A drop fade keeps going: it curves behind the ear and carries on down across the back, so the nape is faded too.

A quick mirror test: check the back of the neck. Longer hair back there points to a burst. Short, faded hair there, with a line that dipped down to meet it, points to a drop.

04 / HEIGHT AND FINISH

Low, mid or high drop.

Where the curve starts.

A low drop starts just above the ear and keeps the most hair on the sides. A mid drop starts around the temple. A high drop begins up near the top of the head and makes the curve dramatic.

Take it to bare skin and you have a drop skin fade, the highest-contrast version. Leave a little length at the bottom and it reads softer.

Ask for it by name and point at the back. 'Drop fade, mid, down to skin' tells me most of what I need, and a photo covers the rest.

06 / QUESTIONS

Drop fade, answered.

What is a drop fade?+

A fade whose line curves down behind the ear and finishes lower at the nape, instead of running level around the head.

Drop fade vs burst fade: what's the difference?+

A burst curves around the ear and leaves the back long. A drop curves behind the ear and keeps fading down the back.

Is a drop fade the same as a taper?+

No. A taper only touches the sideburns and neckline. A drop fade covers the whole side and back, with a line that dips behind the ear.

Does a drop fade work on straight hair?+

Yes. The curve behind the ear shows up on straight hair, waves and curls alike.

Can I add a design to a drop fade?+

Yes. Lines cut into the faded side are a hair design, a $10 add-on to the haircut.
