STYLES / CURLY TOP FADE

Curly top fade.

Curls on top, skin on the sides. A curly top fade lets your natural curl or wave do the work up top while everything below is faded short.

The photo shows it on a younger client: loose curls left with movement on top, and a skin fade that wraps around the ear and across the back. The bare sides make the texture above look even fuller.

02 / WHO IT SUITS

Made for texture.

Curly, coily or wavy.

This is a cut built around hair that already curls. The fade removes bulk from the sides, which is where curly hair tends to puff out, and leaves the top to show its natural pattern.

Straight hair but want more going on up top? The Perm on Booksy is built to add movement and texture to hair that lies flat.

03 / HOW HIGH

Pick the fade height.

It changes the silhouette.

A low fade keeps more hair on the sides and gives a rounder overall shape. Taking it higher, up to skin like the one pictured, puts the curls on a pedestal. A burst fade, curving behind the ear, saves weight for the back, which some people prefer as the curls grow.

05 / UPKEEP

Curls hide growth. Fades don't.

Sides every few weeks.

On top, a few weeks of growth just adds volume. The faded sides tell the real story and usually want a cleanup within three weeks. Between visits, curls stay more defined when you pat them dry instead of rubbing with a towel.

06 / QUESTIONS

Curly hair questions.

What fade should I get if I have curly hair?+

Any height works. Go low for a softer, rounder look, mid or high for more contrast, or a burst if you want to keep length at the back.

How do I deal with frizzy curls?+

Frizz mostly comes from dryness and friction. Rinse with cool water, pat dry, use a leave-in conditioner or curl cream, and try not to touch it once it sets.

Can kids get a curly top fade?+

Yes. Kids are welcome, and they book the Essential Haircut on Booksy like anyone else. The kids' haircuts page has more for parents.

What is a low curly top fade?+

The same curly top with a fade that starts just above the ear, so more hair stays on the sides. It is a gentler version for school or work.
