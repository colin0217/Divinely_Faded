STYLES / TEXTURED CROP

Textured crop.

Short on top, choppy on purpose, worn forward. The textured crop cuts the top so the hair breaks into pieces, then brings it forward into a short fringe over tight sides.

The back view shows a fuller version, with the fringe left heavy and the fade dropping to skin at the nape. The side view is shorter: pushed forward and up, with a squared line-up at the front and a taper around the ear.

02 / CROP VS. FRENCH CROP

Messy or neat.

Two cousins, one shape.

The French crop is the tidy relative: a short, blunt fringe across the front and a neat top. The textured crop is the lived-in one, with a broken-up fringe and more movement. Both sit forward and both take seconds to style, so the choice is about finish, not effort.

03 / WHO IT SUITS

Thick hair loves it.

Finer hair can work too.

Thick, straight or wavy hair that naturally wants to sit forward is the easiest fit. Because the crop brings hair over the front, it can also soften a higher hairline or a larger forehead, which is why it turns up so often in searches about thin hair and receding hairlines.

05 / UPKEEP

Low effort, short cycle.

Fingers and a little product.

Styling is quick: a pea-sized amount of matte paste or clay, rubbed through dry hair and pushed forward. The fade needs a refresh every two to four weeks, and once the fringe starts landing on your eyebrows, the shape is gone.

06 / QUESTIONS

Crop questions.

What is a cropped fringe?+

A short fringe, usually ending well above the eyebrows, cut as part of a crop. It can be blunt and straight or broken up for texture.

Does a textured crop work for thin hair?+

Often, yes. The texture adds visual volume and the forward direction covers more of the front than a style pushed back.

What haircut suits a big forehead?+

Styles that bring hair forward, like a crop or a fringe, cover part of the forehead. Styles that sweep hair back, like a slick back, show more of it.

How short are the sides on a textured crop?+

Your call. A low fade or taper keeps it softer; a higher fade taken to skin makes the textured top stand out more.
