STYLES / SKIN FADE

Skin fade.

A skin fade takes the sides and back all the way down to bare skin, then blends upward into the length you keep on top. There is no hard ledge where the short hair stops, only a smooth change from scalp to hair.

These are skin fades from my chair in Carlsbad. One pairs a short top and crisp line-up with a thick, full beard. The other two show the fade from behind, where the skin wraps around the ear before the hair darkens again.

02 / THE CUT

Down to the skin.

Height is the decision you make.

Every skin fade has a starting point. A low one begins just above the ear and keeps more weight on the sides. A mid skin fade starts around the temple. A high skin fade climbs toward the crown and gives the boldest contrast.

03 / WHO IT SUITS

Clean sides, any texture.

Straight, wavy, curly or coarse.

Clippers treat every hair type the same way, so a skin fade works on straight hair, waves and tight curls alike. It suits anyone happy to show some scalp. Want a little shadow left on the sides? A taper keeps more cover.

05 / UPKEEP

Skin shows growth first.

Plan on a short cycle.

Bare skin is the first part of any cut to change. Within about a week the lowest section starts to shadow over, and by week three the blend has softened noticeably. People who like it sharp usually rebook every two to three weeks. Letting it run longer is fine if you enjoy the grown-in stage.

06 / QUESTIONS

Skin fade, answered.

What is a skin fade at the barber?+

A fade that finishes at bare skin. The sides and back are taken down until no hair is left at the bottom, then blended up into the hair on top.

Is a skin fade the same as a taper?+

No. A taper only touches the sideburns and nape, leaving most of the sides alone. A skin fade wraps all the way around the head and goes to skin.

Can I get a skin fade with a full beard?+

Yes. Book the Signature Cut so your beard is handled in the same appointment as the fade.

Low, mid or high skin fade: which should I ask for?+

Decide how much contrast you want. Low keeps it subtle, mid sits in between, high is the boldest. Still unsure? The side views on this page make a good reference.
