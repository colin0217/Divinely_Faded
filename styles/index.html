03 / FADE HEIGHT

Low, mid, high.

The one choice every fade shares.

Most of these styles start with a fade, and the height is where it begins on the side of your head. Low starts near the ear, mid around the temple, high close to the crown. A drop fade dips down behind the ear toward the nape. Tell me the height when you sit down and the rest follows.
