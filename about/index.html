ABOUT

Tim Murillo.

I'm Timothy Murillo. Most people call me Tim. Divinely Faded is my shop in Carlsbad, New Mexico.

I've been cutting hair since 2023, and I work solo. Every appointment on the books is with me, from the first look at your hair to the last pass of the razor.

BOOK WITH TIM
02 / THE STANDARD

The standard.

Elevate Your Standard.

That's my tagline, and it's how I think about a cut. Precision first. Then the details that make it feel finished: the straight razor work, the hot towel, the clean edge where the fade meets the skin.

Working alone means one person answers for the whole visit. If something isn't right, you know exactly who to tell. My faith is part of that too: whatever I do, I try to do it with all my heart.

03 / IN MY CHAIR

What I do.

Cuts, beards and more.

Haircuts for men and kids, beard trims and straight razor shaves, facials, braids, hair designs, color and perms. Toupee services are coming soon.

The shop is at 303 S Halagueno St, Carlsbad, NM 88220. It's appointment only, and every booking goes through Booksy.
