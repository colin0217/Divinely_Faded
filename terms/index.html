02 / APPOINTMENTS

Appointments.

The shop is appointment only. Every appointment is booked through Booksy, a separate booking service with its own terms.

Booking. Choose your service and time on Booksy. Booksy confirms the appointment and sends its own reminders. You are responsible for the details you enter there, so use a phone number we can reach you at.

Prepayments. Some services ask for a prepayment on Booksy when you book. A prepayment is not an extra charge: it comes off the price of that service when you pay at the shop.

Cancelling or rescheduling. Plans change, and an early cancellation frees the chair for someone else. Cancel or reschedule in Booksy before the deadline shown on your booking confirmation and nothing is charged. After that deadline, or if you do not show up, Booksy’s cancellation policy applies: we may keep the prepayment or charge a cancellation fee through Booksy. Whether it is applied is up to us.

Running late. If you are running behind, text us so your barber can plan. Arriving late can mean a shorter service to fit the time left, and under Booksy’s policy a late arrival may count as a no-show.

Holidays. We are open on holidays at our regular hours. An appointment on the holiday itself carries a $20 holiday fee on top of the service price.

Kids. A parent or guardian books a child’s appointment and stays responsible for the child while they are in the shop.

03 / PAYMENT

Prices and payment.

Prices are listed on this site and on Booksy, and they can change. The price Booksy shows when you book is the price that applies, plus any add-ons you choose at the chair.

Payment is due at the shop when your service is done. We take cash, card, Zelle and Cash App. Any prepayment you made on Booksy is taken off the total.

If something about your cut is not right, say so before you leave the chair, while your barber can still adjust it.

04 / IN THE SHOP

In the shop.

A few ground rules keep every visit safe and on time.

Health and safety. Before we start, tell your barber about any skin condition, allergy, sensitivity or recent chemical treatment, such as color, bleach, a perm or a relaxer, that could affect your service. For everyone’s safety, we may decline or stop a service when a condition could spread, such as lice or an open sore, and ask you to rebook once it has cleared.

Respect. Our barbers and other clients deserve a calm, respectful chair. We may decline or end a service for behavior that is abusive, threatening or unsafe, and we may decline future bookings.

Color, perms and braids. Results depend on your hair’s history and condition. Tell us about past treatments, and understand that chemical services carry some risk even when they are done with care.

Photos and video. We sometimes photograph or film finished cuts for the shop’s portfolio, this website and our social media. Tell your barber before the cut if you would rather not be photographed, and we will respect that. We photograph clients under 18 only with a parent or guardian’s permission.

05 / TEXTS AND CHATS

Texts and chats.

When you text Divinely Faded, or message us through a chat button on Google, we reply only about your question or your appointment. How often you hear from us depends on the conversation. Your carrier’s message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to any text from us and we will stop messaging you; you can also block the number. In a Google chat, use the block or report controls in the chat, or tell us to stop. For help, reply HELP, text or call (575) 706-3095. Booksy sends its own appointment reminders, which you control in your Booksy settings. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle what you send us.
