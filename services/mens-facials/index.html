04 / WHICH ONE

Short on time?

Go Express.

The Express Facial takes 30 min. The Deluxe takes 40 min and costs $40, and that's where the advanced treatments and the serum come in.

If you mostly want the steam and the hot towels with your haircut, look at the Luxury Grooming Experience on the haircuts page. Facial steam and dual hot towels are already built into it.

Either facial books on Booksy like any other appointment. Payment comes after the facial, and skipping the appointment without notice may carry a cancellation fee.

05 / QUESTIONS

Good to know.

How much is a facial?+

The Express Facial is $30, and the Deluxe Facial is $40.

How long does a men's facial take?+

On Booksy, the Express runs 30 min and the Deluxe runs 40 min.

Both include facial massage, warm steam, gentle exfoliation and a hydrating moisturizer. The Express includes a deep cleanse, while the Deluxe includes advanced skin treatments and an anti-aging serum.

Does any haircut come with facial steam?+

Yes. The Luxury Grooming Experience includes facial steam and dual hot towels with the cut.
