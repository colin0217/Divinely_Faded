03 / THE HOT TOWEL

Why the towel.

Warmth before the blade.

A hot towel wrapped around the face softens the beard and relaxes the skin before a razor touches it. You lean back, the towel goes on, and for a minute nothing is asked of you.

Then the razor goes to work.

04 / GO FURTHER

The full beard treatment.

Sculpted, not just trimmed.

The Luxury Grooming Experience includes a beard sculpt along with the haircut, a wash, facial steam, dual hot towels and razor detailing. If your beard is a big part of your look, that's the booking that gives it the most time.

05 / QUESTIONS

Good to know.

On Booksy, a beard trim or line-up is part of the Signature Cut, and the Straight Razor Beard Shave is its own service. For anything else, text me and ask.

No. Beard dyeing isn't something I offer.
