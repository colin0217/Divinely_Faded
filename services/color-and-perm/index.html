Color and perm.

Change more than the length.

Two services for when a new cut isn't enough: a Color Transformation to bleach and tone your hair to a new shade, and a Perm to give it volume, texture and movement it doesn't have on its own.

03 / COLOR

Bleach and tone.

Light takes time.

Going blonde starts with bleach, and toning is what takes it from brassy to the shade you actually wanted. The photos show where it can land: an almost white platinum crop over faded sides, and a lighter blonde through the top of a mid fade.

Booksy blocks 4 hr for a Color Transformation, so plan the whole stretch and don't book anything tight after it.

Color is for the hair on your head. Beard dyeing isn't a service I offer.

04 / PERM

Texture on purpose.

Volume, texture, movement.

A Perm is customized to you. More volume on top, more texture through a crop, movement in hair that lies flat: tell me which one you're after and the perm is set for that result.

It runs 90 min. Bring a photo of the wave or curl you have in mind so we're aiming at the same thing.

05 / CHOOSING

Shade or shape.

Start from the goal.

Book the Color Transformation when the goal is a new shade, and the Perm when the goal is shape and movement. Not sure which one gets you there? Send me a picture of what you have in mind by text.

The bill is settled after the service, like any visit. Both of these hold a long stretch of my day, so if plans change, cancel early on Booksy, since a missed or last-minute cancellation may cost a fee.
