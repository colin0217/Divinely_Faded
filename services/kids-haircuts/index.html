02 / BOOKING

How parents book.

Same app, same chair.

Booksy doesn't list a separate kids' service, so book the haircut that fits what your child wants [TIM?]. The Essential Haircut is $50 and Booksy holds 40 min for it.

I work solo, so your kid sees the same barber every visit. That helps when they're still getting used to the clippers.

If your child has a picture of the cut they want, bring it. It saves guessing on both sides of the cape.

03 / THE CUTS

What's in the photos.

Real cuts from my chair.

A taper with a long, textured top keeps length up front and on the crown while the back and sides stay tight. A low fade with a line-up keeps everything short and squared off. A curly top over a skin fade lets natural curl sit on top of a clean side.

Kids who want lines cut in can add a hair design for $10.
