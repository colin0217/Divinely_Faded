02 / BOOKING

How parents book.

Same app, same chair.

Booksy doesn't list a separate kids' service, so book the haircut that fits what your child wants. The Essential Haircut is $50 and Booksy holds 40 min for it.

I work solo, so your kid sees the same barber every visit. That helps when they're still getting used to the clippers.

If your child has a picture of the cut they want, bring it. It saves guessing on both sides of the cape.

03 / THE CUTS

What's in the photos.

Real cuts from my chair.

A taper with a long, textured top keeps length up front and on the crown while the back and sides stay tight. A low fade with a line-up keeps everything short and squared off. A curly top over a skin fade lets natural curl sit on top of a clean side.

Kids who want lines cut in should see the hair designs page, which has two kids' designs. The design add-on is $10.

04 / PARENTS ASK

Good to know.

Which service do I book for my kid?+

Booksy lists my haircut services with their prices. Book the one that fits what your child wants, and text me if you have a question first.

When can I bring my kid in?+

Any open slot on Booksy. Saturdays run 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM, which helps around the school week. I'm closed Wednesday and Sunday.
