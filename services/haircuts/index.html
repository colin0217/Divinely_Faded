SERVICES / HAIRCUTS

Haircuts.

Three ways to book a cut with me, from a clean haircut to the full grooming treatment. Prices start at $50 for the Essential Haircut.

Each one is tailored to your style and the shape of your head, and each one finishes with straight razor work. What changes is how much happens around the cut: the beard, the hot towels, the wash, the steam.

03 / HOW TO CHOOSE

Which one is yours.

Start with the beard.

No beard, or you keep it up yourself: the Essential Haircut. A beard or line-up you want sharp: the Signature Cut. A visit where you want everything handled in one booking: the Luxury Grooming Experience.

Plan your time around what Booksy holds: 40 min for the Essential, 45 min for the Signature and 50 min for the Luxury.

04 / BRING A PHOTO

Know the style?

A screenshot beats a description.

Low taper, burst fade, skin fade, textured crop, slick back. If you know the name, the style pages show what each one looks like. If you don't, bring a picture and we'll work out the length together before the clippers come out.

Want lines cut in? The hair designs page shows that work, listed on my menu as a $10 add-on.
