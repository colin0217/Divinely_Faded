ARTESIA / HAIR DESIGNS

Hair designs, from Artesia.

A design is a line or curve shaved into the shortest part of a fade. I cut them in Carlsbad, 36 miles south of Artesia on US 285, about 44 minutes by car (OpenStreetMap routing). On Booksy it's an add-on at $10.

Two things change when you're coming from Artesia: who in the car is likely asking for one, and how long it stays sharp before the next drive.

01 / WHO ASKS

Kids first, then the twenties.

Residents under 18 are 31.6% of Artesia, the highest share of the three cities on these pages and well above Carlsbad's 22.6%; another 21.9% are 20 to 34 (Census ACS 2023). A line above the ear is often the first thing a kid asks for once they have an opinion about their hair, and a bolder freestyle curve tends to come from the twenties. Two of the designs on my hair designs page are on young clients.

Book the haircut on Booksy, add the hair design, and if a sibling wants one too, book them back to back so the drive covers both.

03 / HOW LONG IT LASTS

Sharp for a while, then it blurs.

Because the design lives in the part of the fade that's nearly skin, it's also the first thing regrowth softens. From Artesia that matters: the trip back down is 44 minutes, so decide up front whether you want the lines re-cut every visit or you're fine letting them fade out between trips.

If you want them kept crisp, put the next appointment on Booksy before you leave the shop.
