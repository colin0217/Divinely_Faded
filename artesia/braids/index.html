03 / THE HEAT

Rows keep the hair off your neck.

Artesia sees roughly 41 days a year at 100°F or more, with humidity averaging about 38% (Open-Meteo, 2023–25). Braids hold the hair tight to the scalp and a fade underneath leaves the sides bare, so less sits on your neck through a summer like that. Air that dry also dries the scalp under the rows, so oil it between washes and the set lasts better.

04 / SATURDAY MATH

Make it a morning.

Braids plus the round trip is most of a half day. On Saturday I open at 8:00 AM, so the first slot of the morning puts you back on 285 toward Artesia well before the afternoon, with the rest of the weekend untouched.

A missed braids slot is a hard one for me to refill, so if plans change, cancel on Booksy as early as you can. A late cancellation or a no-show may carry a fee.

05 / QUESTIONS

Braids from Artesia, answered.

How much are braids?+

The same from Artesia as from across the street: it varies by style. Text me a photo first and ask.

How long is the whole trip?+

Roughly 44 minutes down, 2 hr in the chair, 44 minutes back. Clear the block.

Can the sides be faded in the same visit?+

Yes. Most sets on the braids page sit over a fade or taper. The fade grows out before the braids do, so the sides may bring you back down sooner than the rows.

How do I pay?+

After the set is finished: cash, card, Zelle or Cash App.
