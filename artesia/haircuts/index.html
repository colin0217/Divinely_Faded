01 / THE WORK WEEK

Cuts that survive a hard hat.

In Artesia, 15.9% of jobs are in mining, oil and gas or agriculture (Census ACS 2023). If that's your week, you want hair that holds under a hard hat and asks nothing of you in the morning: the Essential Haircut, my shortest full service.

Another 21.9% of Artesia is between 20 and 34 (Census ACS 2023). At that age a lined-up beard is often part of the ask, which is the Signature Cut.

Saturday runs 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. If a rotation eats your weekdays, an early Saturday slot gets you cut and back on 285 with most of the day still ahead of you.

04 / FROM ARTESIA

Haircut questions from Artesia.

Which haircut should I book if I work in the oilfield?+

Any of the three. Short and easy: the Essential Haircut. Beard needs lining up too: the Signature Cut. Mention the hard hat when you sit down and we'll talk through how short to go on the sides.

How long is the drive from Artesia?+

About 44 minutes and 36 miles down US 285 to 303 S Halagueno St, Carlsbad, NM 88220.

Can I come after work on a weekday?+

Weekday appointments are Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday; Booksy shows the latest open time each day. Wednesday and Sunday I'm closed.

Do I need an appointment?+

Yes. I only cut by appointment, so grab a time on Booksy before you head down. The haircuts page has the full menu.
