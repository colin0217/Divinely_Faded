01 / TWENTIES AND THIRTIES

The ages growing it out.

21.9% of Artesia is between 20 and 34 (Census ACS 2023), the stretch of life where the mullet has come back as a deliberate cut rather than a dare. I'm not saying it has an age limit. But if you're in that range and thinking about it, start the back growing now: the sides can be faded at any visit, while the tail takes months to pass the collar. Wavy and curly hair gets there with more shape; straight hair needs the extra length first.

02 / THE LONG APPOINTMENT

Platinum earns the trip.

Color Transformation runs 4 hr on my menu, because bleaching and toning to platinum can't be rushed. If you're driving 44 minutes to get here, that's a booking that justifies the mileage. Text me first so we plan cut and color as one visit instead of two trips. For the haircut on its own, the faded sides are what bring you back; the tail only needs shaping now and then while it grows.

04 / QUESTIONS

Mullet questions from Artesia.

How long before a mullet looks like a mullet?+

Starting from short hair, expect a few visits where I keep the sides tight while the back catches up. Several months, depending on how fast your hair grows.

Is a burst fade mullet different?+

The fade curves behind the ear instead of running level, which keeps weight at the nape. Same length in back, a softer outline. See it on the modern mullet page.

Can I do cut and color on the same day from Artesia?+

Text me at (575) 706-3095 before you book and I'll tell you how to line the two up.

Can a mullet pass at work?+

A shorter tail over a low fade reads far more conservative than a long one over high skin. It dials up or down.
