02 / A TOWN FULL OF KIDS

Book the siblings in a row.

This is a cut I do on kids and on men in their twenties and thirties alike, and Artesia has plenty of both: Census ACS 2023 counts 2,735 kids enrolled in K–12, and 21.9% of the town is 20 to 34. So if you're a parent driving down, odds are you're not driving alone. Book each child their own appointment back to back with yours, and everyone leaves with a fresh fade from a single trip. 03 / THE SIDES SET THE PACE

Curls forgive. Skin doesn't.

On top, a few weeks of growth just reads as more curl. The faded sides are where you'll notice the cut aging, since regrowth shows against bare skin. From Artesia, that's the part worth timing: ask me how long the sides will hold, then put the next date on Booksy before the 44-minute drive home. A low fade keeps more hair on the sides and buys a little more room between visits.

04 / QUESTIONS

Curly hair, Artesia questions.

How young can my kid be for a curly top fade?+

If they can sit still for the clippers, I'll cut it. Parents pick the haircut on Booksy; it's the same menu as adults.

Is one appointment enough for two kids?+

No. Each child needs their own booking so I have the full time for each cut. Book them back to back.

What fade height do you suggest for school?+

Low keeps it soft and grows out gently. Higher gives more contrast. The curly top fade page shows a high one.

Can I get curls if my hair is straight?+

A perm adds the texture first. Text me at (575) 706-3095 before booking so we plan it right.
