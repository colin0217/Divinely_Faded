02 / TOP AND SIDES

The top stretches. The sides don't.

A slick back is forgiving on top; more length often combs back easier than less. What dates it is underneath, where the fade or taper puffs out as it grows. So the sides decide when you drive down again, and a low taper will give you a longer gap than a high skin fade. Tell me which matters more, the sharpness or the spacing, and I'll cut to it. Then book the next one before the 44 minutes home.

03 / WORK AND WEATHER

An office cut in an oilfield town.

Artesia's median age is 30.9 and 15.9% of its jobs are in mining, oil and gas, or agriculture (Census ACS 2023). A slick back is the opposite of a hard-hat cut: it wants a comb and a minute in the morning. If your week is split between the field and somewhere you need to look sharp, say so, and I'll keep the top short enough to push back by hand on the days a helmet goes on.

The climate helps. Humidity sits around 38% (Open-Meteo, 2023–25), and combed-back hair tends to hold in dry air far better than in muggy weather, where it lifts by lunch. You still need something in it, but you're not fighting the sky.

04 / QUESTIONS

Slick back, Artesia questions.

How much length do I need on top before I drive down?+

Enough to lie back without standing up, longest at the front. If it's not there yet, I can cut the sides now and shape the top as it grows.

Fade or taper under a slick back?+

A skin fade makes it sharper and more modern; a low taper keeps it classic and stretches the time between visits.

Can I add a shaved line?+

Yes. The second cut on the slick back page has one curving behind the ear.

Will my curly hair slick back?+

With a shorter top, or as a looser pushed-back shape. Bring a photo of what you're after.
