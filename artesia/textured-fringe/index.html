01 / A YOUNG TOWN

Half of Artesia is the fringe's age.

31.6% of Artesia is under 18 and another 21.9% is 20 to 34 (Census ACS 2023): put together, more than half the town sits in the ages where a forward fringe lives. It's a look that reads young on purpose. If you're a parent booking one, it's the same Essential Haircut adults get, in its own slot on Booksy, and a photo of the exact fringe your kid wants will save us both a conversation. 02 / THE FRONT IS THE TIMER

When it hits the eyes, it's due.

A fringe sets its own schedule: once the front is in your eyes, the shape has gone. The taper underneath usually wants attention a little before that. From Artesia that's worth planning, so ask me how many weeks the front has in it and book the next visit on Booksy while you're still in Carlsbad, rather than noticing it on a Monday morning 44 minutes from the chair. Day to day there's no routine: push it forward by hand and go.

04 / QUESTIONS

Fringe questions, Artesia.

Textured fringe or edgar for my son?+

An edgar runs a blunt, straight line across the forehead over a sharp fade; a textured fringe is softer and choppier at the ends. Bring the photo and we'll match it.

Can two kids share one booking?+

No. Each haircut needs its own slot so I have the time. Book them back to back and make one drive of it.

Does the fringe need a comb in the morning?+

No. Push it forward by hand. That's the whole routine.

Who shouldn't get a fringe?+

Anyone who hates hair on their eyebrows, or has a strong cowlick at the front that pushes hair up instead of forward. The textured fringe page shows three weights of it.
