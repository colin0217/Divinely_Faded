VISIT / FROM ARTESIA

Coming from Artesia.

My shop is in Carlsbad, not Artesia. It sits about 36 miles south, and it's US 285 almost the whole way: roughly 44 minutes from the center of Artesia to my door.

If that drive is worth it to you, this page is how to plan it, so the trip ends in my chair at the time you booked.

GET DIRECTIONS
01 / PLAN THE DRIVE

Book first, then drive.

I only take appointments, so pick your time on Booksy before you leave Artesia. Every open slot shows up there, and booking one holds your chair.

Saturday runs from 8 AM to 2 PM, which helps if you're tied up through the week. Weekday appointments are Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday. Wednesday and Sunday I'm closed.

Give yourself the full 44 minutes plus a little room for traffic once you're in Carlsbad. If something holds you up on the road, text me at (575) 706-3095 so I know where you're at.

Booksy may charge a fee for a no-show or a late cancellation. If your plans change, cancel in the app as early as you can.

05 / QUESTIONS

Before you head down.

How far is Divinely Faded from Artesia?+

About 36 miles, or roughly 44 minutes south on US 285. The shop is at 303 S Halagueno St, Carlsbad, NM 88220.

Do I need an appointment if I'm driving in?+

Yes. I work by appointment only, so book on Booksy before you leave and your chair is held for that time.

Is Saturday a good day to come down?+

Saturday is 8 AM to 2 PM, which suits anyone who works through the week. Booksy shows which Saturday times are still open.

Can my kids come too?+

Yes, kids are welcome. Book a slot for each of them on Booksy so the whole family gets done in one trip.

How do I pay once I'm there?+

After your cut, pay however suits you: cash, card, Zelle or Cash App.
