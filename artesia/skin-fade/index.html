ARTESIA / SKIN FADE

Skin fade, from Artesia.

Sides and back shaved clean to the scalp, blending into whatever length stays on top, with no step anywhere in the change. My shop is in Carlsbad, 36 miles south of Artesia on US 285; OpenStreetMap routing puts the trip at about 44 minutes.

Two things decide whether this is your cut: the weather you live in, and how you feel about regrowth on bare skin.

01 / DRY HEAT

Bare sides for an Artesia summer.

July in Artesia averages a high of 97°F, and the year brings about 41 days at 100°F or hotter, with relative humidity around 38% (Open-Meteo, 2023–25). That's dry heat. With the sides at skin there's no hair to trap it against your head, which is why some people switch to this cut once the hundreds start and keep it until October. Carlsbad, at 98°F, isn't any cooler, so the drive doesn't buy you a break either.

02 / THE COMEBACK

Regrowth shows at the bottom first.

Bare skin is where the first few days of growth appear, as a shadow over the lowest band. Nobody else sees it as soon as you do. If you like it razor sharp, you'll want to come back sooner than a taper would ask; if you don't mind a grown-in look, stretch it. From Artesia, with 44 minutes each way, I'd pick the gap together in the chair and put the next date on Booksy before you leave.

03 / QUESTIONS

Skin fade, Artesia questions.

Low, mid or high skin fade for the heat?+

Height is about contrast, not temperature; all three go to skin at the bottom. Pick by how bold you want the sides.

How long is the drive from Artesia to your chair?+

About 36 miles and 44 minutes, straight down US 285 to 303 S Halagueno St, Carlsbad, NM 88220.

Does a skin fade work on curly hair?+

Yes, on curls, waves and straight hair alike, since the sides come all the way off. The skin fade page shows it next to a full beard.

Should the beard come off for summer too?+

That's up to you. If it stays, book the Signature so it's lined up in the same visit as the fade.
