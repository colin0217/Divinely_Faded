01 / THE AIR

Dry, and not much rain to fix it.

Artesia averages about 38% humidity and only around 8.1 inches of rain a year (Open-Meteo, 2023–25). Skin in that air gives up moisture all day and gets little back. A facial reverses some of it: warm steam opens things up, gentle exfoliation clears what the wind left behind, and the moisturizer at the end is the step that matters most for the drive home.

If your days are spent outside, in the 15.9% of Artesia jobs that are mining, oil and gas or agriculture (Census ACS 2023), add dust and sun to that list. The Deluxe, with its advanced skin treatments and serum, is the one I'd point you to.

03 / ONE TRIP

Pair it with the cut.

The sensible way to do a facial from Artesia is to book it back to back with a haircut on Booksy, so one drive covers both. Or book the Luxury Grooming Experience, which already builds facial steam and two hot towels into the cut.

A facial books like anything else here: appointment only, paid after, and a no-show or late cancellation may cost a fee.

04 / QUESTIONS

Facial questions from Artesia.

Is the drive worth it for a facial alone?+

Your call. The Express is 30 min and the Deluxe is 40 min, so pairing one with a haircut makes the 44-minute trip go further.

Which facial for dry skin?+

Both end with a hydrating moisturizer. The Deluxe adds advanced treatments and a serum as well, which is why I'd pick it for skin that's been in the sun and wind.

Can I get the steam without a full facial?+

It does: facial steam and a pair of hot towels are part of the Luxury Grooming Experience, alongside the cut.

What does a facial cost?+

The Express Facial is $30; the Deluxe Facial is $40. The men's facials page breaks down each step.
