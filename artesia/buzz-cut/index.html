ARTESIA / BUZZ CUT

Buzz cut, from Artesia.

A buzz cut is one short clipper length everywhere, with a fade and a line-up so it looks finished rather than grown out. I cut them at 303 S Halagueno St, Carlsbad, NM 88220 in Carlsbad, which from Artesia is 36 miles south along US 285, roughly 44 minutes (OpenStreetMap routing).

Not a long drive, but long enough that you'll want the cut to earn it.

01 / WORK WEEKS

Short enough for a hard hat.

Census ACS 2023 puts 15.9% of Artesia's jobs in mining, oil and gas, or agriculture. If you're one of them, a buzz asks nothing of you on an early start: no comb, no product, nothing a helmet can flatten. It's also part of why Saturday morning appointments are on my calendar, for anyone whose weekdays belong to a shift.

03 / HOT DAYS

Less hair for a long summer.

Artesia sees about 41 days a year at or above 100°F, with July highs averaging 97°F (Open-Meteo, 2023–25). Some people go to a buzz for exactly those months and let it grow back in the fall. Others never let it get longer. Either way, the fade and the front line are what blur first, so they set your return date, not the top.

04 / QUESTIONS

Buzz cut questions from up the road.

How often will I drive down from Artesia for a buzz cut?+

It depends on how sharp you keep the fade. Ask me in the chair; I'll give you a gap that fits your hair, and you can book it on Booksy before leaving.

Crew cut or buzz cut for a job site?+

Both work under a hard hat. A crew cut keeps a bit more on top to comb; a buzz is the same length all over and needs nothing.

Can I bring my kid for a buzz at the same time?+

Yes. Book your child their own slot on Booksy right after yours; kids are welcome.

Do you shave heads too?+

I do. The Head & Beard Shave takes everything to skin with hot towels. The buzz cut page shows where a high and tight stops short of that.
