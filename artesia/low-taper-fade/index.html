ARTESIA / LOW TAPER FADE

Low taper fade, from Artesia.

A low taper trims only the edges: the sideburns, the hair around the ear and the neckline. The sides above keep their length, so the cut looks tidy from the front and never grows a hard line. I cut it in Carlsbad, 36 miles south of Artesia on US 285, about 44 minutes each way by OpenStreetMap routing.

Of all the fades on my menu, this is the one that asks the least of that drive.

01 / FEWER TRIPS

A fade that doesn't rush you back.

With so little skin on show, a low taper fuzzes over slowly. The nape is the first spot to lose its edge, and even that is subtle. For someone 44 minutes away, that means a longer stretch between visits than a skin fade would give you. A mid taper, starting up near the temple, shows growth sooner and wants a slightly tighter schedule; I'll tell you which one you're looking at before I start.

03 / SUN, SCHOOL AND WORK

Cover where you want it.

July highs in Artesia average 97°F, and about 41 days a year reach 100°F (Open-Meteo, 2023–25). A skin fade is one answer to that. A taper is the other: if you'd rather not have bare scalp out in that sun all summer, it keeps hair on the sides and still cleans up the edges.

It's also the fade I'd point a parent toward. Nearly a third of Artesia is under 18, 31.6% by Census ACS 2023, more than Carlsbad's 22.6%, and a taper passes a school dress code without a shaved stripe up the side. Same test at a conservative job. Kids book the regular haircut services on Booksy, each in their own slot.

04 / QUESTIONS

Taper questions, Artesia edition.

Low taper or mid taper if I only drive down now and then?+

Low. It keeps more hair on the sides, so the regrowth is softer and the cut stays presentable longer between trips.

Is a low taper a good first fade for my son?+

It's the gentlest one I do. Nothing goes to skin unless you ask, and it grows out without a step.

Can I keep my hair long on top with a taper?+

Yes. The taper only touches the edges, so the top can be a crop, a fringe or combed back. The low taper fade page shows a longer top.

How do I know when it's time to come back?+

When the back of your neck starts looking fuzzy in the mirror. That's the taper's signal.
