01 / BOOKING & HOURS

When and how.

Do I need an appointment?+

Yes. Divinely Faded is appointment only, and every booking goes through Booksy. Pick your service, pick your time, and you're set.

What are your hours?+

Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday from 9:00 AM to noon and again from 12:30 to 5:00 PM [TIM?]. Saturday from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

Are you open on Saturday?+

Yes, 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. It's the one weekend day I cut.

Are you open Sunday or Wednesday?+

No. The shop is closed every Wednesday and every Sunday.

Are you open on holidays?+

Yes, on my normal schedule for that day of the week. If you book on the holiday itself, there's a {{holiday_fee}} holiday fee on top of the service.

How do I reach you?+

Text (575) 706-3095. I'd rather get a text than a call, but calls work too.

02 / PRICES & PAYMENT

What it costs.

How much is a haircut?+

The Essential Haircut is $50. The Signature Cut, which includes a beard trim or line-up and a hot towel finish, is $65. The Luxury Grooming Experience is $80. Every service and add-on is listed on the services page.

What payment do you take?+

I take cash, card, Zelle and Cash App. You pay in full after your service, not before.

What happens if I miss my appointment or cancel late?+

No-shows and late cancellations may be charged a cancellation fee through Booksy. If something comes up, cancel on Booksy as early as you can.

03 / IN THE CHAIR

What to expect.

Do you cut kids' hair?+

Yes, kids are welcome. Book them on Booksy the same way you'd book yourself.

How long does each service take?+

Essential Haircut 40 min. Signature Cut 45 min. Luxury Grooming Experience 50 min. Straight Razor Beard Shave 40 min. Head & Beard Shave 1 hr. Express Facial 30 min. Deluxe Facial 40 min. Braids 2 hr. Perm 1.5 hr. Color Transformation 4 hr.

What should I ask for?+

A photo of the cut you want does most of the talking. Tell me how short you want the sides, how much to leave on top, and whether the beard needs work, because a beard trim or line-up means booking the Signature Cut.

How long do braids take?+

Booksy holds 2 hr for braids, and the price varies with the style you choose.

Do you dye beards?+

No. Beard dyeing and capillary treatments aren't on my menu. Toupee services are coming soon.
