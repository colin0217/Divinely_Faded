# Divinely Faded: keyword → page map (EN + ES)

Generated 2026-10-24 from the saved keyword research (`keyword-research-2026-10-24/`) by `build/seo-agent/v2/build-v2.mjs`. Every number below is looked up from those files; nothing is typed by hand.

## Rules

- **One owner per keyword.** Every keyword (EN and ES, compared exactly and after dropping stopwords, folding plurals and sorting words) belongs to exactly one page or post. The generator fails if any page or blog post shares a primary or a secondary with another.
- **Pages own commercial and local intent** ("burst fade carlsbad nm", "beard trim near me"). **Blog posts own questions, comparisons and how-tos** that no page answers in its own section. Where a style page already has a section on a comparison (low taper vs mid taper, crop vs French crop, buzz/crew/high-and-tight, burst fade mullet), the page keeps it and no post targets it.
- **ES primary rule.** Eddy County Spanish planner term for the page's intent; if Eddy shows only a 10/mo floor long-tail variant and a head phrase has >=10x its US-es volume, the head phrase is primary and the Eddy variant a secondary; no evidence = natural phrase, low/no data.
- EN page primaries are the ones the built pages already target (`seo.primary` in `content/en/*.json`). The geo-modified term usually has no planner volume of its own, so each row also shows the head term's numbers.

Sources:
- **eddy**: Google Ads Keyword Planner ideas, Eddy County NM, English; 12-month average (pulled 2026-10-24). 10/mo is the planner's floor bucket.
- **eddy_es**: Google Ads Keyword Planner ideas, Eddy County NM, Spanish language setting; 12-month average. 10/mo is the floor bucket.
- **carlsbad**: Google Ads Keyword Planner ideas, Carlsbad NM (city).
- **us**: Google Ads Keyword Planner, United States, English, 12-month average (pulled 2026-10-24).
- **us_es**: Google Ads Keyword Planner, United States, Spanish language setting (pulled 2026-10-24). The planner does not split volume by language: an English string returns the same number under es (e.g. 'taper fade' 246,000 in both), so only Spanish phrases measure Spanish demand.
- **gbp**: Divinely Faded Google Business Profile search terms, Oct 2025 to Sep 2026 (12-month totals; '<15' = Google's privacy threshold).
- **kd**: Keyword difficulty 0-100 (clickstream-modelled, US English), pulled 2026-10-24.
- **autocomplete**: Google autocomplete suggestions for Carlsbad barber seeds (EN + ES).
- **masked**: Google withholds volume for many children/youth terms (planner and clickstream both return nothing). Proxy: 'corte de pelo para niños' 2,400/mo US-es and autocomplete.

## Spanish vocabulary the data supports (for the glossary, C1)

- **Fade = "desvanecido"** (Mexican Spanish): "desvanecido bajo" 2,900/mo US-es vs "degradado bajo" 590 and "fade bajo" 880. Eddy County Spanish searches use "desvanecido" too. Write "desvanecido" first, "degradado" second.
- **Curly hair = "pelo chino"**: "pelo chino hombre" 720, "cortes para pelo chino hombre" 260 (and in Eddy County data). "Rizado" also works ("corte de pelo rizado hombre" 480).
- **Highlights = "rayitos"**: "rayitos para hombre" 720 vs "mechas hombre" 90.
- **Straight hair = "pelo lacio"**; **hard part = "corte con raya"**; **fringe = "fleco"** ("flequillo" also appears in Eddy data).
- Trend names stay English: low taper, burst fade, buzz cut, skin fade, curly top, French crop.
